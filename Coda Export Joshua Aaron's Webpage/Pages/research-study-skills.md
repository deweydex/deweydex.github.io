Introductory Task: 

On a new word doc please answer the following questions and go through the process below:

1. What are three surprising or useful facts that you carry around with you? If you can’t think of any how would you go about finding some? What sites or tools would you use?
2. Where did you learn these facts? If you wanted to verify them, how would you?

Task: try to verify the facts that you have specified. What are your sources, how do you know that that source is credible or “good”? Do you find any conflicting sources?

Homework: After you have answered those questions, please write a 150-250 word reflection on what was easy or difficult about this these questions and this task: what was unexpected? How did you verify your source? If you had to challenge or critique how you went about answering those questions, what would you say?

The Humble Banana

Dear students,

In this class, we will be discussing framing, more importantly we will be discussing a highly contentious topic with broad and deep societal and historical significance: Bananas. This topic has too often been viewed as banal, mundane, quotidian, and other less fancy words for “everyday.” Together, we will approach this topic from four different frames, in an attempt to better understand not just the role of the humble banana in our society, but also how the way we frame facts changes their power, their relevance, and their scope. We will discuss argument, form, and tone, as well as investigating both what sources are easily accessible and what frames those resources contain within them. 

For today’s class, we will be going through this assessment and answering any questions or concerns you might have. For now, there is no due date or assessment criteria for you to meet, we will discuss those next week (when my voice is better). But for now, lets just go through together and have a look.

Your tasks for today:

1. take out a laptop and sign in
2. have a look at the four frames, pick a frame and let me know what you are interested in doing so we have an equal number for each one
3. Start working on research and writing!
