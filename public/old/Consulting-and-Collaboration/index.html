For Colleagues

This section is for people who want to understand how I work, not just what I teach.

If you're a teacher or educator — whether you're thinking about collaborating, curious about how I run workshops on AI, or just interested in comparing notes on teaching mathematics or programming to adult learners — you'll find the most relevant starting point in How I Think About Teaching. The consulting page also covers the workshop and professional development work I do with teaching staff.

If you're an administrator or employer — evaluating my work for a role, thinking about contracting, or trying to get a sense of my background and approach — Working With Me is the right place to start. It covers my experience, what kinds of work I take on, and how to get in touch.

The two audiences overlap more than you'd expect. The teaching philosophy that shapes my classroom work is the same one behind the consultancy, the workshops, and the curriculum design. If you read one thing before reaching out, it's this.
