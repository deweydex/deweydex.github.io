AI for Business (2025–26)

QQI Level 5 module at DCFE. Weekly class notes 2024–25. Covers AI/ML tools in professional contexts, prompt engineering, and critical evaluation of AI systems.

Contact: joshuaaaron@dcfe.ie

Final week

Review sheet on Moodle.

Weeks 9–11: Structured Prompts — Pair Querying Activity

Task: create 3 structured prompts using the Goal / Format / Tone / Context framework, then compare outputs across different AI models (Gemini, Claude, ChatGPT, Deepseek).

Prompt structure:

Goal — What you want the tool to do, create, find, or answer.

Format — What form should the response take? Length, sources, structure, examples.

Tone — How should it sound? Who is the audience?

Context — What information does the model need to do the task well? (Don't include personal details like address, phone, or bank info.)

Each of your 3 prompts should be significantly different across all four categories.

Week 8: Comparing Official Prompting Guides

Find the official prompt guides for the following (from the company that made them — not from the models themselves):

  1. Claude (Anthropic)
  2. Gemini (Google)
  3. ChatGPT (OpenAI)
  4. Deepseek (Deepseek)
  5. Perplexity (Perplexity)

Compare and contrast them in a document. Consider: how are they structured? What do they recommend or discourage? Are they aimed at end users or developers?

Key terms identified in class: Context, Tone, Audience, Recommendations, Format/Structure, Goal.

Week 6: Prompt Guides — Professional Writing

Compose 2 distinct multiline prompts for Gemini (or another LLM) to create a personal LinkedIn statement or another professional-facing text (cover letter, etc.).

Include all three features: Context (who is this for?), Format (structure of response?), Tone (how should it sound?).

Example worked through in class: a cover letter prompt providing job description, CV bullet points (without personal contact details), and a target tone.

Week 5: Finding Prompting Guides

Set up accounts for Claude.ai, Gemini, and ChatGPT. Find the official prompt guide for each.

Prompting first steps:

  • LLMs are not search engines — add context, preference, and structure.
  • Context: why are you asking, for whom, in what situation?
  • Format: how should the response be structured?
  • Tone: how should it sound? What feeling should it give the reader?

Week 4: Accounts and First Prompts

Set up accounts: - LinkedIn (personal account + add college email) - Gemini (Google account) - Claude (sign in with Google)

Introduction to the Context / Format / Tone prompting framework.

Week 3: AI and ML Tools — Investigation

Investigate everyday applications that use AI/ML using three lenses: right context for use, potential difficulties and risks, safe and ethical engagement.

Tools explored: Google Docs, Google Sheets, Grammarly, LinkedIn, Coda, Notion, Canva, Figma.

Key questions for each tool: What does the AI portion claim to do? How easy is it to opt out? What data is collected and where does it go?

Homework video: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LPZh9BOjkQs

Week 2: Baselines

Explored AI text transformation tools: Summarize, Shorten, Bullet Points, Convert to Table, Simpler Language, Elaborate.

Week 1: Introduction

Course overview: three forms of machine learning — dispelling myths, addressing biases, developing facility and literacy around how ML systems work, how they're sold, and how they're depicted in media.
