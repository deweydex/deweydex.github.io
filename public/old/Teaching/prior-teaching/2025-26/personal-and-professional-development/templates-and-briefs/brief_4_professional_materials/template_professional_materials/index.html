Component 1: CV Requirements

Required Sections

Your CV must include the following sections:

  • [ ] Contact Information

  • Name

  • Phone number

  • Email address (professional)

  • LinkedIn URL (if applicable)

  • Location (city/country)

  • [ ] Professional Summary or Objective (2-3 sentences)

  • Who you are professionally

  • What you are looking for

  • What you offer

  • [ ] Education

  • Institution names

  • Qualifications/degrees

  • Dates (month/year)

  • Relevant coursework (optional)

  • [ ] Experience

  • Job titles or roles

  • Organizations

  • Dates (month/year)

  • 3-5 bullet points per role describing achievements

  • Quantify results where possible (numbers, percentages, outcomes)

  • [ ] Skills

  • Technical skills relevant to the role

  • Software/tools you know

  • Languages spoken

  • Organized logically

Formatting Requirements

  • [ ] Maximum 2 pages

  • [ ] Consistent formatting throughout

  • [ ] Professional font (Arial, Calibri, Times New Roman, 10-12pt)

  • [ ] Clear section headers

  • [ ] Appropriate use of bold, italics, bullets

  • [ ] White space for readability

  • [ ] No spelling or grammar errors

  • [ ] Saved as PDF with filename: LastName_FirstName_CV.pdf

Component 2: Cover Letter Requirements

Structure

Your cover letter must follow this structure:

  • [ ] Header

  • Your contact information

  • Date

  • Employer contact information

  • Professional greeting

  • [ ] Opening Paragraph (2-3 sentences)

  • State the position you are applying for

  • How you found the position

  • One sentence about why you are interested

  • [ ] Body Paragraph 1 (3-4 sentences)

  • Why this specific role interests you

  • What you know about the company/organization

  • How your goals align with their mission

  • [ ] Body Paragraph 2 (3-4 sentences)

  • What relevant experience you bring

  • Specific examples of your qualifications

  • How you meet key requirements from the job posting

  • [ ] Closing Paragraph (2-3 sentences)

  • Thank them for their consideration

  • Express interest in discussing further

  • Professional closing and your typed name

  • [ ] I can explain and defend everything in my materials

Notes on Using AI Tools

If you use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) for brainstorming or suggestions:

  • [ ] I used AI only for ideas, not to generate complete materials

  • [ ] The final materials are written in my own voice

  • [ ] I understand everything in my materials

  • [ ] I can explain and discuss my experience without referencing my documents

  • [ ] My materials sound like me, not like AI

  • [ ] I have customized any AI suggestions significantly

Remember: Employers can recognize generic AI-generated content. Your materials must be authentically you.
