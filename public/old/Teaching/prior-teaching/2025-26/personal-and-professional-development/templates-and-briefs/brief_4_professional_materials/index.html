Brief 4: Professional Materials

Assessment Brief 4: Professional Materials Package

Weight: 20% of final grade
Submission Deadline: [To be announced]

Overview

Develop complete application materials for a real job, internship, or opportunity that genuinely interests you. This assessment builds practical professional communication skills you will use throughout your career.

What to Submit

Create a complete application package including all four items:

1. CV (Curriculum Vitae)

  • Maximum 2 pages

  • Clear, professional formatting

  • Tailored to the specific role

  • All sections properly labeled

2. Cover Letter

  • Maximum 1 page

  • Addressed to real organization

  • Explains why this role, why this organization, and why you

  • Professional business letter format

3. LinkedIn Profile Screenshot

  • Professional photo or avatar

  • Compelling headline

  • Complete About section

  • Experience and education visible

4. Job Posting

  • Screenshot or PDF of actual job posting

  • Must be real, current job listing

  • Shows job title, organization, and description

Use the provided template: template_professional_materials.md

Grading Criteria

CV Content and Formatting (3%)

  • 3 points: Exceptional CV, perfectly formatted, clearly tailored to role

  • 2 points: Strong CV with good formatting and clear tailoring

  • 1 point: Adequate CV meeting basic requirements

  • 0 points: Incomplete or poorly formatted

Cover Letter Content (3%)

  • 3 points: Compelling letter making strong case for candidacy

  • 2 points: Strong letter addressing key questions

  • 1 point: Adequate letter meeting basic requirements

  • 0 points: Generic or incomplete

LinkedIn Profile (2%)

  • 2 points: Professional, complete profile supporting application

  • 1 point: Adequate profile with required elements

  • 0 points: Incomplete or unprofessional

Overall Package Quality (2%)

  • 2 points: Highly consistent, professional package

  • 1 point: Generally consistent package

  • 0 points: Inconsistent or incomplete

Professional Communication (4%)

  • 4 points: Exceptional writing quality, perfect tone and style

  • 3 points: Strong writing with appropriate tone

  • 2 points: Adequate writing with minor issues

  • 0-1 points: Poor writing quality or inappropriate tone

Audience Awareness (3%)

  • 3 points: Expertly tailored to role and organization

  • 2 points: Well-tailored to role

  • 1 point: Some tailoring evident

  • 0 points: No tailoring or generic content

Self-Presentation (3%)

  • 3 points: Exceptional positioning of skills and experience

  • 2 points: Strong positioning

  • 1 point: Adequate positioning

  • 0 points: Poor or unclear positioning

Note on AI Tools

You may use AI tools (ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) for brainstorming and suggestions. However:

  • Final materials must be in your own voice and sound like you

  • You must understand and be able to explain everything in your materials

  • Generic AI-generated content is easily recognized by employers

  • Using AI does not make this easier - it just changes what the work looks like
