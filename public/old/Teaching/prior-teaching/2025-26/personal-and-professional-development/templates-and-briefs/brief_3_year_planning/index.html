Brief 3: Year Planning

Assessment Brief 3: Rest of Year Planning and Strategy

Weight: 25% of final grade
Submission Deadline: [To be announced]

Overview

Create a practical plan for managing your remaining college deadlines, exams, and projects. This assessment helps you develop time management and stress management skills while planning for actual upcoming challenges.

What to Submit

1. Written Plan Document (10%)

A 3-4 page plan that includes:

  • Complete Calendar: All deadlines, exams, and major projects across ALL modules

  • Weekly/Monthly Breakdown: What you need to focus on each period

  • Time Management Strategy: Specific strategies for balancing everything

  • Stress Management Plan: How you will handle exam period and crunch times

  • Specific Goals: What you want to achieve for each remaining assessment

  • Contingency Plans: What you will do if things do not go as planned

2. Video or Presentation (15%)

A 3-5 minute video or presentation explaining:

  • Your overall strategy for the rest of the year

  • What you are most worried about and how you will handle it

  • What you have learned about your own work patterns

  • One key change you are making based on what you have learned

Format options: Recorded video, screen recording with voiceover, or live presentation in class

Use the provided template: template_year_planning.md

Grading Criteria

Written Plan Components (10% total)

Complete Calendar (2%):

  • 2 points: Comprehensive calendar covering all modules with accurate dates

  • 1 point: Mostly complete calendar with minor gaps

  • 0 points: Incomplete or missing

Weekly/Monthly Breakdown (2%):

  • 2 points: Detailed, realistic breakdown showing clear priorities

  • 1 point: Basic breakdown with some detail

  • 0 points: Superficial or missing

Time Management Strategy (2%):

  • 2 points: Specific, practical strategies with realistic time allocations

  • 1 point: General strategies with some specifics

  • 0 points: Vague or unrealistic

Stress Management Plan (2%):

  • 2 points: Concrete strategies with specific actions

  • 1 point: General strategies mentioned

  • 0 points: Vague or missing

Goals and Contingencies (2%):

  • 2 points: Clear goals and thoughtful backup plans

  • 1 point: Goals stated with minimal contingency planning

  • 0 points: Unclear or missing

Presentation Components (15% total)

Presentation Clarity (5%):

  • 4-5 points: Exceptionally clear, well-organized presentation

  • 3 points: Clear presentation with good organization

  • 2 points: Adequate clarity with some organization issues

  • 0-1 points: Unclear or poorly organized

Strategic Thinking (5%):

  • 4-5 points: Demonstrates sophisticated planning and prioritization

  • 3 points: Shows good strategic thinking

  • 2 points: Shows basic strategic thinking

  • 0-1 points: Limited strategic thinking evident

Self-Awareness (5%):

  • 4-5 points: Deep understanding of personal patterns and growth areas

  • 3 points: Good self-awareness with specific insights

  • 2 points: Basic self-awareness

  • 0-1 points: Limited self-awareness
