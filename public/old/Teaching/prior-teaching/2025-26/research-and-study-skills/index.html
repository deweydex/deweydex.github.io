Semester 2 overview (January–April)

Week of March 9

Discussion of study types where standard "good research" methods are impossible:

  1. Cancer treatment — when a placebo is a death sentence, what do we compare instead?
  2. Car safety — how do you control for behavioral variables like mode of transport?
  3. Invisible classes in research — e.g., studying whether to lower the voting age to 16. What are the downsides of each approach? Where do values intersect with method?

Week of March 2

Draft of final brief released. Feedback welcome. Briefs are a chance to demonstrate knowledge and process, not a test by torture.

Week of February 23

Forces, incentives, and ideologies that influence research methods and articles:

Sample size, distribution, questionnaire framing, placebos/nocebos/bias, when a blind trial is impossible, how framing shapes how we read a study, age of study, comparison studies.

Week of December 1

Drafting week. General feedback:

  • Include at the top of your draft: What frame did you choose and why? Who is your audience and why? What format did you choose and why?
  • Fewer words in a format (poster, short social media post) means harder choices, not easier ones.
  • Include source references inline so the reader can see when you're drawing on a specific fact or idea.
