2 Tool Limitations Filled

Building a Hermeneutic of Suspicion: A Critical Evaluation Exercise - Answer Key

Modern chatbots like ChatGPT, Claude, and Gemini are complex hybrids that combine search, generation, and retrieval. Understanding what each component does well and poorly helps us evaluate outputs critically.

Important: These are tendencies, not absolutes. Any given prompt might produce a good answer due to the hybrid nature of modern systems. The goal is to understand underlying limitations, not to memorize rules.

Part 1: Prompts That Challenge Search Engines

Prompt Why This Challenges Search Engines
Write a letter of recommendation for a student applying to nursing school Requires creation, not retrieval
Explain why the protagonist in this novel might have made that choice Requires interpretation beyond text
Create a lesson plan for teaching fractions using real-world examples Requires synthesis and creation
What would happen if we increased the minimum wage to €20/hour? Requires reasoning about hypotheticals
Summarize the main arguments from these three articles and identify common themes Requires analysis across sources

Part 2: Prompts That Challenge Pure LLMs

Prompt Why This Challenges Pure LLMs
What did the Taoiseach say in yesterday's press conference? Specific recent event post-training
What are the current mortgage interest rates in Ireland? Current market data, changes daily
How many students are enrolled at Dundrum College this year? Institution-specific information
What's the weather forecast for Dublin this weekend? Real-time meteorological data
Did the Luas workers vote to strike? Recent vote results

Part 3: Prompts That Challenge RAG Systems

Prompt Why This Challenges RAG
This source says X but this other source says Y - which is correct? Requires adjudicating between sources
Summarize the consensus view on this topic (when sources disagree) Need judgment when sources conflict
What should I do about this ethical dilemma? (cites ethics codes but can't make judgment) Can cite codes, can't make judgment
Is this scientific claim legitimate? (retrieves both good and bad sources) Retrieval doesn't equal evaluation
What's the best approach to teaching reading? (retrieves conflicting pedagogies) Can retrieve claims, not arbitrate

Part 4: Prompts That Challenge Modern Hybrid Systems

Prompt What Could Go Wrong?
Quote the exact text from page 47 of the 2023 QQI guidelines May hallucinate precise citations
What percentage of Irish secondary students reported using AI tools in the most recent nationwide survey? May confuse or invent statistics
Cite three peer-reviewed studies published in 2024 about AI in education May retrieve irrelevant studies or invent plausible-sounding ones
What did Maria Montessori write about the role of technology in learning? May attribute modern concepts to historical figures
How many teachers attended the last ETB professional development conference? May invent plausible but false numbers
Calculate the exact correlation coefficient between class size and test scores based on the Department of Education's 2023 data May perform calculation incorrectly or retrieve wrong data
What is the current policy on AI use at Dublin City University? May retrieve outdated or hallucinate current policy
Find me the original source of this widely-cited statistic about student engagement May not find original source or accept secondary claims
What were the specific recommendations from the Oireachtas committee report on digital literacy? May retrieve partial info or synthesize incorrectly
Explain the relationship between these three different theoretical frameworks (requires synthesis) May create false coherence between incompatible frameworks

Developing Critical Evaluation Habits

When evaluating AI outputs, ask yourself:

  • What kind of information or capability would this task require?

  • Where might that information come from? (Training data, retrieval, nowhere?)

  • What could go wrong? (Hallucination, misinterpretation, outdated info?)

  • Does this require judgment that goes beyond pattern matching or retrieval?

  • Would I be able to verify this answer? How?

The Hybrid Complication

Modern chatbots are unpredictable because they're hybrids that may route queries differently. This unpredictability is why we need critical evaluation habits rather than simple rules about what works and what doesn't.
