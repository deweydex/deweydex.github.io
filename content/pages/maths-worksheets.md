I wrote these worksheets for learners who have a complicated history with mathematics and who now want to use it in programming and machine learning. Each one asks many short questions, then asks the learner to explain what they noticed. The questions grow from routine practice into puzzles and real uses, so a teacher can stop at the point that suits the class.

A PDF is the version to print. A TeX file is the source, and TeX is a typesetting language for mathematics that you can open in any LaTeX editor, such as Overleaf, and change to fit your own class. Answer keys are not on this page. If you teach with these worksheets and want one, write to me from the contact section on the front page.
