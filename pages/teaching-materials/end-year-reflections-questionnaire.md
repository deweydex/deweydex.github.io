Learning Student Backgrounds

In the first weeks of my classes I spend a good deal of time learning the level, academic background, and emotional connections of my students in reference to the subjects I am teaching. Sometimes this takes the form of in-class informal poll, other times I use questions (and forms) like those below to be able to get a lot of quantitative and qualitative data quickly and easily.

Questionaire for Students



Questions for All Students:

What is your program (course) of study here?




What led you to choose that program and this school?




What would you like to do once you finish the program?




What are some things you are thinking of doing longer term professionally? (It is totally fine if you don’t know or if you haven’t decided on a specific path. In that case, if you had your druthers (everything goes your way) where would you be and what would you be doing longer term?




What are some of your interests? I can start to get the ball rolling! I love to read, I am a fan of fancy coffee and tea and the cafes that serve them, I’m an avid woodworker, I love dark chocolate and mint and fresh baked crusty bread and vegetarian cooking, I recently dove into the world of house-plants, but had to give them away when we moved so I am back to just a couple cuttings. I mostly listen to Jazz and Classical Music with a few more popular things thrown in. I love hiking, I’m a long time meditator, I used to dance and will hopefully get back into that this year (swing, salsa, bachata, cha-cha, etc.), I generally love to learn and educational youtube is my guilty pleasure, I relish all things related to books: audio books, physical books, libraries, bookstores and more. In terms of subjects I am a pretty broad reader, everything from poetry and philosophy to astrophysics and immunology. I tend to like when examples are practical, but not “everyday,” when they make me learn something beyond just what we are covering. 




Have you lived anywhere other than Dublin? If not, where in Dublin? If so, where? I’ve lived in quote a few cities throughout my life, but I always marvel at those who have deep roots in a place.




Do you speak any other languages besides English? (Personally I speak German as well as a bit of a few other languages)




How was your educational experience so far? Did you find you connected with school? Did you have any great teachers? Did you meet good friends at school? Have you studied other subjects in the past?




Do you have a laptop that you can bring to class and use for school work?




How comfortable would you say you are with:




Word/Google Docs, Excel/Sheets…




What parts of a computer can you name? I’ll start you off: we have the monitor/screen, the chassis or body of the computer, and then we have what is inside, what are some things we might find inside a computer? How might choosing different internals affect how the computer runs?




Outside of school, when you have to learn something new, where do you go? What resources do you use?




If you had to learn math or programming outside of a course like this, where would you turn? What resources would you use? Are there particular youtubers or websites that you would try out?
