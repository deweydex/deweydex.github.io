Week 3: Elaboration on AI and ML Tools

Before delving into the intricacies of how Artificial Intelligence (AI) and Machine Learning (ML) operate, it's essential to explore various tools that we interact with daily. Many applications we use unknowingly leverage AI and ML technologies. When examining these tools, we can adopt different perspectives: understanding the appropriate context for their usage, identifying potential challenges and risks, and ensuring ethical and safe engagement with these tools.

In the exploration of these applications, several questions and considerations may arise:

* What functionalities does the tool offer, and how does it achieve them?
* What type of data is collected by the tool?
* How is this data utilized to enhance user experience and outcomes?

The following tools or applications utilize AI/ML in diverse ways to assist users in accomplishing a range of tasks. While creating accounts on these platforms is optional for this course, it can be beneficial for advancing your professional objectives.

Key Questions:

1. What does the AI portion of this tool claim to do?
  1. How fast or easy is it to use?
  2. How fast or easy is it to correct when a mistake is made?
  3. How fast or easy is it to opt-out (not use) this tool?
2. What kind of information or data does the AI portion of this tool give me?
  1. What kind of resources does this tool have access to?
  2. How does what I write factor into what is produced?
  3. What kind of options does this tool give me when I use it?
3. What kind of information is collected?
  1. Where does that data go?
  2. How is my data used?
  3. How can I opt out (or in) to this data collection?
