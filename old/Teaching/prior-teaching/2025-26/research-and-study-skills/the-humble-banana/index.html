The Humble Banana

The Humble Banana Assignment (Brief 1 — 35%)

Research and Study Skills, 2025–26. This was the first major assignment of the year.

The idea

We explored a topic with broad and deep societal and historical significance: bananas. The banana is too often viewed as mundane and everyday — in this assignment, we approached it from four different frames to understand how framing changes the power, relevance, and scope of facts. We investigated argument, form, tone, accessible sources, and the frames those sources contain.

Pick your frame

  1. Big Banana — the industry, economics, and corporate history of banana production
  2. Bananas For All — access, food justice, and who benefits from banana trade
  3. Badnana — environmental and ecological concerns around monoculture and pesticides
  4. BAN-Bananas — political and historical dimensions of banana republics and colonialism

Assignment submission

  • A draft of a piece of media for your chosen frame (submitted via Moodle for feedback)
  • A reflection documenting your process, sources, and challenges (and joys)
  • A final version incorporating feedback from the draft

For the reflection, include at the top: what frame you chose and why, who your audience is and why, and what format you chose and why.
