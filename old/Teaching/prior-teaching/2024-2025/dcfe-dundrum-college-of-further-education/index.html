Dublin College Dundrum — Prior Teaching (2024–25)

Dublin College Dundrum — 2024–25

Four courses during the 2024–25 academic year, all QQI Level 5. Involved building several of these courses largely from scratch while teaching them simultaneously.

Courses

  • Foundations of AI for Business — A non-technical course on AI/ML tools for business students. Covered tool literacy, critical evaluation, prompt design, and the distinction between genuine AI capabilities and hype. Used LinkedIn Learning as a supplementary resource.

  • Maths for IT — QQI Level 5 Mathematics for IT (5N18396). Taught September through March, covering algebra, functions, calculus, trigonometry, probability and statistics, and algorithms. Python used throughout for visualisation and applied problems.

  • Web Authoring — Fundamentals of HTML/CSS and web publishing for computer science students.

  • Programming Design Principles — Python programming focused on structured thinking, modular design, and collaborative problem-solving. Included the Game Map project as an informal introduction to applying programming to real-world spatial problems.

This year was also split with Dublin College Blackrock (formerly BFEI) — see the BFEI section for those materials, including the cross-module integration work.
