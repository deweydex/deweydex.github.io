Planning for Assignment

First Big Choice

  • Personal website that can be part of your portfolio
  • Website about something you are interested in that you can be proud of

Planning

In a separate document, outline the following. Once you have completed your outline of each of these you can start gathering or creating your content.

  • Overarching goal and topic of your webpage
  • Sketch or wireframe of the layout
  • What do you actually want to say? How is the content structured and broken down into pages?
  • Page connection graph: how are pages connected to each other?
  • Potential tools or issues to think through before you start
