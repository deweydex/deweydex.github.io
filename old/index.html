Josh Aaron

I'm a teacher and consultant based in Dublin. I teach programming, mathematics, and AI at further education level — currently at Dublin College Dundrum. Previously I've taught at Dublin College Blackrock (formerly BFEI), the Nelson Mandela School in Berlin, and worked with Paper in Toronto as part of their tutor management team.

This site collects my teaching materials, guides, and occasional writing. It's a work in progress and probably always will be.

Teaching — Course content, notes, guides, and resources for current and past students. If you're in one of my classes this year, find your materials under Current Teaching. The Guides section covers how to approach programming and mathematics more generally. External links are organised by subject under Resources.

Consulting — I work with schools, colleges, and organisations on curriculum design, AI literacy workshops, and professional development for teaching staff. See what I offer or go straight to getting in touch.

For Colleagues — How I think about teaching and background on my experience for educators and employers.

Writings — Occasional writing on teaching, mathematics, and programming. A work in progress.
