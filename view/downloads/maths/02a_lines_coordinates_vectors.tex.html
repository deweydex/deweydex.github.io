\hypertarget{part-a-coordinate-basics-10-problems}{%
\subsection{Part A: Coordinate Basics (10
problems)}\label{part-a-coordinate-basics-10-problems}}

\textbf{1.} Plot the following points on a coordinate plane and label
each with its coordinates: - A = (3, 4) - B = (-2, 5) - C = (-4, -3) - D
= (5, -2) - E = (0, 3) - F = (-3, 0)

\textbf{2.} In which quadrant (I, II, III, IV) or on which axis does
each point lie? - (a) (7, -2) - (b) (-3, -8) - (c) (0, 5) - (d) (-4, 6)
- (e) (2, 0)

\textbf{3.} Find the distance between each pair of points: - (a) (0, 0)
and (3, 4) - (b) (1, 2) and (4, 6) - (c) (-2, 3) and (4, -1) - (d) (-5,
-2) and (3, 4)

\textbf{4.} Find the midpoint of the segment connecting: - (a) (2, 8)
and (6, 2) - (b) (-3, 4) and (5, -2) - (c) (0, 7) and (-4, -1)

\textbf{5.} A point starts at (2, 3) and moves 4 units right and 5 units
down. What are its new coordinates?

\textbf{6.} The midpoint of segment PQ is (3, 1). If P = (7, 4), what
are the coordinates of Q?

\hypertarget{part-b-vectors-12-problems}{%
\subsection{Part B: Vectors (12
problems)}\label{part-b-vectors-12-problems}}

Vectors are written in bold: \textbf{v}, \textbf{w}, \textbf{u}. They
have both magnitude (length) and direction.

\textbf{7.} Given \textbf{v} = (3, 4) and \textbf{w} = (1, -2),
calculate: - (a) \textbf{v} + \textbf{w} - (b) \textbf{v} - \textbf{w} -
(c) \textbf{w} - \textbf{v} - (d) 2\textbf{v} - (e) -3\textbf{w} - (f)
2\textbf{v} + 3\textbf{w}

\textbf{8.} Find the magnitude (length) of each vector. Recall:
\textbar{}\textbf{v}\textbar{} = √(v₁² + v₂²) - (a) \textbf{v} = (3, 4)
- (b) \textbf{w} = (5, 12) - (c) \textbf{u} = (-2, 2) - (d) \textbf{p} =
(1, 1)

\textbf{9.} A unit vector has magnitude 1. Find the unit vector in the
same direction as: - (a) \textbf{v} = (3, 4) - (b) \textbf{w} = (0, 5)

\emph{(Hint: divide each component by the magnitude)}

\textbf{10.} Vector \textbf{a} = (4, 3) and vector \textbf{b} = (-3, 4).
- (a) Calculate \textbf{a} + \textbf{b} - (b) Calculate
\textbar{}\textbf{a}\textbar{} and \textbar{}\textbf{b}\textbar{} - (c)
Calculate \textbar{}\textbf{a} + \textbf{b}\textbar{} - (d) Is
\textbar{}\textbf{a} + \textbf{b}\textbar{} equal to
\textbar{}\textbf{a}\textbar{} + \textbar{}\textbf{b}\textbar? Why or
why not?

\textbf{11.} A drone is at position (2, 5). It moves by vector
\textbf{v} = (3, -2), then by vector \textbf{w} = (-1, 4). What is its
final position?

\textbf{12.} The zero vector is \textbf{0} = (0, 0). For any vector
\textbf{v}: - (a) What is \textbf{v} + \textbf{0}? - (b) What is
\textbf{v} + (-\textbf{v})? - (c) What vector \textbf{w} satisfies
\textbf{v} + \textbf{w} = \textbf{0}?

\textbf{13.} Given points P = (1, 2) and Q = (4, 6), find the vector
\textbf{PQ} that goes from P to Q.

\emph{(Hint: \textbf{PQ} = Q - P)}

\textbf{14.} If \textbf{v} = (2, 5) and \textbf{w} = (k, 15) are
parallel (same direction), what is k?

\begin{center}\rule{0.5\linewidth}{0.5pt}\end{center}

\hypertarget{part-c-slope-and-linear-equations-14-problems}{%
\subsection{Part C: Slope and Linear Equations (14
problems)}\label{part-c-slope-and-linear-equations-14-problems}}

\textbf{15.} Calculate the slope between each pair of points: - (a) (0,
0) and (4, 8) - (b) (1, 3) and (5, 11) - (c) (2, 7) and (6, 3) - (d)
(-3, 2) and (4, 2) - (e) (5, 1) and (5, 9)

\textbf{16.} Without calculating, identify the slope as positive,
negative, zero, or undefined: - (a) A line going uphill from left to
right - (b) A line going downhill from left to right - (c) A horizontal
line - (d) A vertical line

\textbf{17.} Convert to slope-intercept form (y = mx + b): - (a) 2x + y
= 7 - (b) 3x - 2y = 12 - (c) x + 4y = 8 - (d) 5x - y + 3 = 0

\textbf{18.} Find the equation of the line with: - (a) Slope 3 and
y-intercept -2 - (b) Slope -½ and y-intercept 4 - (c) Slope 0 and
y-intercept 5

\textbf{19.} Find the equation of the line passing through: - (a) (0, 3)
with slope 2 - (b) (2, 5) with slope -1 - (c) (4, -1) with slope ¾ - (d)
(-2, 6) with slope 0

\textbf{20.} Find the equation of the line passing through both points:
- (a) (1, 2) and (3, 8) - (b) (0, 5) and (4, 1) - (c) (-2, -3) and (4,
9)

\textbf{21.} Find the x-intercept and y-intercept of each line: - (a) y
= 2x - 6 - (b) 3x + 4y = 12 - (c) y = -½x + 3

\textbf{22.} Line A has equation y = 3x + 1. Line B has equation y = 3x
- 4. - (a) What are their slopes? - (b) Are they parallel,
perpendicular, or neither? - (c) Will they ever intersect?

\textbf{23.} Line A has slope 2. What is the slope of a line
perpendicular to A?

\textbf{24.} Find the equation of the line: - (a) Parallel to y = 2x + 1
and passing through (3, 5) - (b) Perpendicular to y = 3x - 2 and passing
through (6, 1)

\hypertarget{part-d-systems-of-linear-equations-12-problems}{%
\subsection{Part D: Systems of Linear Equations (12
problems)}\label{part-d-systems-of-linear-equations-12-problems}}

\textbf{Solve each system by substitution or elimination. State if
there's no solution or infinitely many.}

\textbf{25.} \[y = 2x + 1\] \[y = -x + 7\]

\textbf{26.} \[x + y = 10\] \[x - y = 2\]

\textbf{27.} \[2p + 3q = 13\] \[p - q = 1\]

\textbf{28.} \[3m + 2n = 12\] \[6m + 4n = 24\]

\textbf{29.} \[2r + 5s = 11\] \[4r + 10s = 15\]

\textbf{30.} \[\frac{t}{2} + u = 5\] \[t - 2u = 4\]

\textbf{31.} Three coffees and two teas cost €11. Two coffees and three
teas cost €12. Find the price of each.

\textbf{32.} A rectangle's perimeter is 28 cm. Its length is 4 cm more
than its width. Find the dimensions.

\textbf{Graphical Interpretation:}

\textbf{33.} Without solving algebraically, sketch these lines and
estimate their intersection point: \[y = x + 1\] \[y = -2x + 7\]

\textbf{34.} What does it mean graphically when a system has: - (a)
Exactly one solution? - (b) No solution? - (c) Infinitely many
solutions?

\textbf{35.} The lines y = 2x + 3 and y = 2x - 1 form a system. Without
graphing or substituting, explain why there's no solution.

\textbf{36.} Write a system of two equations that has: - (a) The
solution (2, 5) - (b) No solution - (c) Infinitely many solutions
